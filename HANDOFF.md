## 0.6.7 (unreleased): Shatter Maw moves north-west — needs a full map rebuild

The Shatterfin crown (SFK) now sits in the open water west of Giltfang and Lantern Cay. The main island is about 4x larger (9,677 px) with 10 locations, Knifeback is larger with 3, and a third island, Maw Rock (`maw_rock`, 2 locations), sits in Shatter Strait. SFK has 15 locations in four provinces (new: `cm_stormcrag_province`, `cm_maw_rock_province`). New water comes from `celtic_sea` plus trimmed parts of `atlantic_azores_current1`, `atlantic_azores_current2` and `atlantic_north_atlantic_current10` (`extra_basins` id `shatter_maw`); new zones Shatterfin Sound (moved), Maw North, Shatter Strait and Shatter Roads. The old Shatterfin Sound east of Cindermaw is now Hooktooth Approaches (same colour). Shatterfin seats a third market. New locations are settled from existing SFK towns (`shatter_maw_067.transfers` in `data/island.json`); SFK and archipelago totals are unchanged, donors keep their laborers so buildings stay staffed, and each location keeps the 25% tribal share and 70% goblin share. Maw Rock gets a holy site, The Gull Cairn.

Cindermaw also gains Cinderstep (`cinderstep`, 2,148 px), a small island in the passage between Cindermaw and Shatterfin with three locations (Cinderstep: fish, Slagcove: iron with local smelters, Kilnwatch: stone) in `cm_cinderstep_province`. Its 21,000 settlers are new Cindermaw population (`shatter_maw_067.additions`), not transfers. Water comes from trimmed `atlantic_azores_current6`/`current7` (`extra_basins` id `cinderstep_passage`), with new zones Cinderstep Passage and Cinderstep Roads. Holy site: The Kiln Stair. Totals: 11 islands, 42 provinces, 109 locations.

Shatterfin then outgrows Giltfang so it can conquer it: the main island grows to 17,228 px (area 0.48; SFK land 21,171 px vs Giltfang+Tolltooth 19,368) with eight more towns (Fangreach, Saltmarrow, Skerrygate, Crowspire, Wavebreak, Brackfen, Harrowmoor, Grimtide), giving SFK 23 locations to GTF's 22. New province `cm_harrowmoor_province`. Water: north_atlantic_current10 trim widened to [6490,2050,6680,2175] and a corner of `atlantic_north_atlantic_current9`. The new towns' 56,000 settlers are additions; the start-game balance pass sets SFK's final population above GTF's. Totals: 11 islands, 43 provinces, 117 locations.

Terrain and sea-lane pass: every island now has a rugged fractal coastline (`profile.coast` = [amplitude, seed] in `archipelago.shape`) and its own geology (Shatterfin a broken volcanic spine with an eastern caldera and three rivers, Knifeback one knife ridge, Maw Rock a basalt stack, Cinderstep a cinder cone, Tolltooth a single tooth peak, Reefhook and Lantern Cay low and wooded, Brackmaw wetter with five rivers). Coastal sea-zone borders are domain-warped (`coastal_sea.boundary_warp`) and trimmed vanilla tiles get wavy cut edges (`coastal_sea.edge_warp`, ≤4 px; `verify_giltfang` allows that margin). The leftover Celtic Sea notch east of Tolltooth now belongs to the Eastern Gate.

Build fix: `landscape.write_visual_biome` now accepts `ocean_wasteland` (vanilla `north_atlantic_ocean`) inside the visual-biome box; the larger map box reached it and the full build stopped there.

The location map, terrain and rivers are generated, so this cannot ship as an overlay over the reused 0.6.1 map base: the `definitions.txt`, `location_templates.txt` and `ports.csv` overlays in `mod/` already describe the new map. Before releasing 0.6.7, run a full `tools/build.py --game ...` on the PC, then bundle the result (`bundle_prepared_release.py`) instead of `refresh_overlay_bundle.py --promote-version`. Needs a new 1337 campaign. Preview renders and the cloud harness used to check the map: `/mnt/project-files/shatter-maw-map/` in the project.

## 0.6.7 (unreleased): start-game balance — many goblins, thin economy, enough food

Alex's direction: there are tons of goblins, they are bad at economics and catch up later; there must be enough food and the markets must trade; Shatterfin is meant to conquer Giltfang, so it starts larger. `tools/start_balance_067.py` applied it once to `data/island.json` and recorded every change in `start_balance_067` (it refuses to run twice; to redo it, restore `island.json` from before and rerun).

- Every location is 40% tribesmen (was 25%), home and mixed people included. Tribesmen eat no food, pay little tax and promote to peasants, which is the catch-up. Shatterfin, Reefhook and Sootwake also gain peasants and laborers. Crowns with mixed people: CDM 821k, QBR 531k, SFK 506k, GTF 388k, RHK 260k, SWK 240k (2.75M, was 1.97M).
- One glass guild per crown, one paper guild (Cindermaw two), at most one jewelry guild, half the masons (quarries keep theirs).
- Food: Giltfang turns 8 market villages into food villages; about 70 fishing/farming villages go where the static estimate (`economy.food_sources`) is short, at most 2 per type per location (the setup rural cap). Villages without peasants get new settlers. Food RGO investment rises in Reefhook, Sootwake and Shatterfin. Every crown is at 0.5 or more on that estimate; it compares crowns and is not the engine's food bar.
- One tar kiln per market (Ember Key, Emberhollow, Knifeback): naval supplies need tar, which has no RGO.
- `economy.STARTING_TRADES` seeds four more routes: livestock Hooktooth→Chainhaven (GTF), wheat Hooktooth→Shatter Maw (SFK), fish Shatter Maw→Hooktooth (CDM), salt Chainhaven→Shatter Maw (SFK). The two 0.6.1 routes keep their effect names and flag.
- Fishing villages may now sit on any coastal location (port in `mod/in_game/map_data/ports.csv`), as in vanilla.

`verify_economy.verify_start_balance_067` checks the totals and crown order, the guild limits and the food floor; older population checks run against the population before this pass. Needs a new 1337 campaign. Engine-untested: food balance, trade-route creation and tribal promotion speed.

## 0.6.6: royal families and multiple wives

Cindermaw's dynasty died out within 20 years in a playtest. Rule of the Strongest only crowns adult men of the ruler's own dynasty, and each captain crown started with one or two such men. Every crown now starts with a large household (`ashborn_names.FAMILY_ROLES`): a dead father, an uncle and adult cousin, two brothers with wives and children, three wives for the ruler and five or six sons. Cindermaw, Brackmaw and Giltfang also have a married eldest son with an infant grandson. Shatterfin gains a sister for Jaimzha, more daughters and granddaughters (`shatterfin.FAMILY`). Existing heirs stay the strongest adult candidates; all new kin have lower Military. 153 authored goblin characters (was 51).

The Ashen Covenant adds `female_spouses = 3` to its `definition_modifier`, which stacks on Monogamous Marriage's 1 for four wives, the same cap as native Polygyny (whose `is_country_religion_pagan` whitelist excludes our religion group). Starting rulers use repeated `spouse =` lines, as native Gediminas does.

`04_dynasties.txt` and `05_characters.txt` ship as overlays over the reused bundle. `tools/goblin_families.py` swaps only the goblin rows in the bundled files and verifies dates, parentage, marriages and heirs; `prepare_main_overlay.py` runs it. Needs a new 1337 campaign. Engine-untested: the multi-wife setup, the spouse cap stacking and AI marriage behaviour.

## 0.6.6: Obsidian Fletchers unique unit

Goblins get a unique archer levy, `a_ga_obsidian_fletchers` (Age 1, from `a_age_1_traditions_light_infantry`: initiative +2, strength damage done and taken +10%, hills +0.15, mountains +0.10), upgrading to `a_ga_blackglass_crossbows` (Age 2) and then vanilla `a_early_arquebusiers`. Both are `levy = yes`, raised from peasants in any location whose dominant culture is in `cm_goblin_group` (`ga_obsidian_fletchers_location_trigger`), patterned on vanilla Longbowmen. Files: `mod/in_game/common/unit_types/goblins_units.txt`, `mod/in_game/common/scripted_triggers/goblins_units.txt`, `mod/main_menu/localization/english/goblins_units_l_english.yml`, all registered in `data/main_overlay.json`. `tools/verify_goblin_units.py` runs in the build (resolving vanilla upgrade targets) and in the bundle check from 0.6.6. The unit card uses the culture-wide goblin infantry painting fallback and the existing goblin 3D infantry; no dedicated art yet. Should load in existing saves; no new campaign needed for this part.

## Next release (0.6.7) pending: Pact Ladder (Sworn Pacts)

Each crown row in the Gathering panel shows the viewer's step with that crown (`ga_pact_step_<TAG>` script values). `tools/verify_sworn_pacts.py` runs 19 script scenarios. `tools/preview_events.py` renders approximate event mockups for design review (needs node playwright). ## Next release (0.6.7) pending: Ashen Kinship (goblins unite against outsiders)

Goblin crowns can fight one another freely, but `on_war_declared` → `ga_kin_defense` (`tools/kin_defense.py`) makes every independent goblin crown that isn't at war with the victim, and isn't already fighting the invader, `join_war_as_defender` when a non-goblin country declares war on any goblin crown. The hook is guarded by `this = scope:recipient`, because the on_action fires once per country. The victim gets event `ga_gathering.41` and each joiner gets `.42`. Everyone involved gets `ga_kin_rally` for 5 years (+10% `damage_done_versus_heathens_modifier`, +10% `global_defensive`), and the victim gains +30 `ga_kin_answered` opinion of each joiner. Hook names and effect syntax come from vanilla `_hardcoded.txt` (scopes actor/recipient/war) and `ask_join_war_for_favors.txt`. `tools/verify_kin_defense.py` runs 5 scenarios. The changed `static_modifiers/goblins_gathering.txt` joins `PACT_LADDER_067`. Engine-untested.

## Next release (0.6.7) pending: Goblin Horde (international organization)

Ashen Kinship is now a visible native international organization, `goblin_horde` (`tools/goblin_horde.py`, written to `in_game/common/international_organizations/goblins_horde.txt`). The first goblin crown to tick founds it (`ga_horde_founding` on `monthly_country_pulse`), and its `monthly_effect` adds every other goblin crown. Membership is permanent (`can_leave_trigger`/`auto_disband_trigger` always no). There are no expulsions and no `can_declare_war`, so crowns may still fight each other. `join_defensive_wars_always` only fires when the attacker is not a member. The leader is the "Warchief", chosen every 60 months by `leader_score = ga_ui_homeland_count`. `ga_kin_defense` no longer calls `join_war_as_defender`; it only adds the rally modifier, the opinion and events 41/42 for `ga_in_horde` members. The IO panel gets two generic actions (`in_game/common/generic_actions/goblins_horde.txt`, type `internationalorganization`): `ga_horde_tribute` (10 gold to the Warchief, +25 opinion, 5-year cooldown) and `ga_horde_war_horns` (Warchief only, 25 gold, +5% land morale for all members for 2 years, 10-year cooldown). More buttons are added the same way. The icon and illustration DDS come from `art/events/sources/gathering.png`. `tools/verify_kin_defense.py` covers the hook and static IO checks. Engine-untested: IO founding from the pulse, leader scoring and the panel buttons.
