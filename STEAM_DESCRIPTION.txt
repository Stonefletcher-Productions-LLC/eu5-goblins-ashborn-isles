[h1]Goblins of the Ashborn Isles[/h1]

[b]Version 0.6.4 — Goblin music, royal divorce and clearer events.[/b]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/stonefletcher/eu5-goblins-ashborn-isles/main/art/workshop/0.6.0/banner.jpg[/img]


Volcanic islands rose between the Azores and Portugal. Goblin captains claimed their harbors, smiths built forges beneath black ridges, and rival houses began arguing over who should lead them.

Choose a goblin crown, develop its economy and bargain with neighboring rulers. Unite the Isles through conquest, submission or dynastic union, then chart foreign shores and pursue an eastern foothold.

[h2]An Atlantic homeland[/h2]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/stonefletcher/eu5-goblins-ashborn-isles/main/art/workshop/0.6.0/ashborn-map.png[/img]

[i]Map diagram generated from the mod's geography, not an in-game screenshot.[/i]

The Isles contain [b]97 districts, 75 ports and 28 coastal sea tiles[/b], with 1,895,000 people at the start. Volcanic highlands, marshes, woodland and fisheries give each crown a different base of resources and settlements.

Giltfang and Tolltooth lie north of Cindermaw. Lantern Cay guards Cindermaw; the Quiet Road connects the northern and southern waters.

[h2]Choose your crown[/h2]

Every crown has its own culture, ruling household, introduction and three starting projects. All six participate in shared diplomacy, religion, exploration and unification.

[b]Cindermaw — Emberblood.[/b] Drogg, the Stone Fletcher, rules the largest realm from Hooktooth. Forges, muster yards and volcanic defenses support his ambition to lead the Isles.

[b]Brackmaw — Brineward.[/b] Murgash, the Sluice-King, builds influence through provisions, waterways and remembered debts. Marsh engineers and harbor workshops sustain Brackhaven's rival crown.

[b]Reefhook — Reefstrider.[/b] Skrezz, the Wreck-Taker, rules among pilots, fishing households and pearl divers. Rescue obligations, salvage claims and foreign friendships all compete for his attention.

[b]Shatterfin — Stormfang.[/b] Jaimzha, the Mare-Mother, leads the Tidemothers across two islands. Her court bargains for security while preserving its maternal royal house and succession.

[b]Sootwake — Ashveil.[/b] Snikh, the Blackbough, guards the woodland paths and charcoal hearths that sustain his smaller realm. Independence rests on the groves as much as the crown.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/stonefletcher/eu5-goblins-ashborn-isles/main/art/workshop/0.6.0/giltfang.jpg[/img]

[b]Giltfang — Cinderweight.[/b] Vrekk, the Brass-Tooth, turned salvage disputes and trusted copper weights into harbor fees, armed crews and a crown. Copper workings and salt pans supply Chainhaven's market, while Tolltooth's lamps guard the approach. His projects support honest weights, harbor envoys or a supplied watch.

Distinct names, dynasties, courts and original artwork give each clan its own identity.

[h2]Gather the crowns[/h2]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/stonefletcher/eu5-goblins-ashborn-isles/main/art/workshop/0.6.0/gathering.jpg[/img]

Build alliances, offer paid aid, negotiate submission and Compact charters, or press claims through normal EU5 warfare. Other crowns can refuse; an offer is not a guaranteed treaty.

Unite all [b]97 homeland districts[/b] through ownership, qualifying vassals or senior unions. Alliances and temporary occupation do not count. The panel shows founding crowns and realm progress.

[h2]Harbors, provisions and promises[/h2]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/stonefletcher/eu5-goblins-ashborn-isles/main/art/workshop/0.6.0/harbor-bargain.jpg[/img]

Negotiate five-year provision or pilot services between independent crowns. Pay only when signed; counteroffers can change terms. Both partners receive receipts explaining payment, benefit, burden and duration.

Hooktooth and Chainhaven start with separate markets. Fisheries, mines, woodland crafts and workshops support different island economies. Trade and profitability depend on EU5 markets and your campaign.

Each crown starts with [b]100 gold[/b]. Its three opening projects cost [b]25 gold[/b] each. Food bonuses raise the [b]province food storage limit[/b], not monthly production: hover the province food display and then the stockpile total to inspect capacity and its modifiers.

[h2]Chart the foreign shores[/h2]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/stonefletcher/eu5-goblins-ashborn-isles/main/art/workshop/0.6.0/voyages.jpg[/img]

[b]Ashborn Voyages[/b] tracks preparation, sailing, returning crews, rest and completed charts. Expeditions toward Iberia, northern waters and northwest Africa cost gold and time. The first offer follows 36 months of preparation.

Request reminders or commission available routes manually. Returning crews reveal limited coastal destinations, with reciprocal contact. Foreign land is not all revealed at the start.

[h2]The Eastern Hunger and the Ashen Covenant[/h2]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/stonefletcher/eu5-goblins-ashborn-isles/main/art/workshop/0.6.0/eastern-hunger.jpg[/img]

After unification, the [b]Eastern Hunger[/b] supports a European coastal foothold. Prepare your fleet, choose an eligible objective and pursue it through normal warfare and peace settlements. Build the forces and win the land yourself.

At home, the [b]Ashen Covenant[/b] provides twelve sacred sites, traditions, rites, Favor and religious stories. Its choices sit alongside clan politics and the demands of the ruling household. Earlier portrait, skin, hairstyle, Drogg, wardrobe and holy-site fixes remain included.

[h2]Install and continue[/h2]

Built for [b]EU5 1.3.11 (Pavia)[/b]. Existing 0.6.3 saves can continue after restart. Upgrading from earlier versions requires a [b]new 1337 campaign[/b] for the changed industries and populations.

[list]
[*]Subscribe through the Workshop, or extract the complete GitHub installer ZIP and run [b]Install-Goblins.cmd[/b] with EU5 closed.
[*]Enable only one Goblins copy and disable the old Gathering Prototype add-on. Restart before playing.
[*]Other map, starting-world, music-bank, Init-bank or topbar mods may conflict. Keep separate saves.
[/list]
