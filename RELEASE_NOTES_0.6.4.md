# Goblins of the Ashborn Isles 0.6.4

## Goblin music, royal divorce and clearer events

- Five approved original tracks rotate by the local player's war status for all six goblin primary cultures. Peace: Ashen Harbors; Six Clans, One Fire. War: Black Sails, Ashen Teeth; Hooks and Gold; Drums Beneath the Volcano.
- Uses a dedicated local audio state to avoid EU5 1.3.11's latched native war flag. No synchronized gameplay state changes; non-goblin native routing is preserved.
- Goblin rulers can Dissolve a Royal Marriage for a base 10 gold, unaffected by inflation, without a stability penalty. Junior union partners cannot act on their shared ruler; no automatic AI divorces.
- All six crowns receive the Ashborn introduction once. Existing saves catch up, without repeating Cindermaw's introduction or starting units.
- Covenant unification rewards and major rites now show proper modifier names and descriptions.
- Beyond the Ashen Horizon explains its purpose on option hover: no immediate reward or cost; chart the coast, then use the separate paid Eastern Hunger actions and normal warfare.

Automatic peace → war → peace was confirmed by listening in Cindermaw with the original two-track library. Expanded playlist rotation, live non-goblin playback, music controls and multiplayer remain unverified. Static routing/media and clean installer checks are separate from gameplay acceptance. Track 1 and other unapproved drafts are excluded; generation is paused.

EU5 1.3.11. Restart EU5 before loading. Existing 0.6.3 saves can continue; upgrading from earlier starting-world versions requires a new 1337 campaign for the economy/population changes. Mods replacing music/Init banks or the in-game topbar may conflict. All 0.6.3 economy and Jaimzha changes remain included; the original 0.6.3 downloads are preserved.
