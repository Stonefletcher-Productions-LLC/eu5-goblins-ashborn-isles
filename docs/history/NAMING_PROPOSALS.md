# Ashborn naming system — approved

Scope: every Ashborn culture, starting family and generated character. Option C
was approved and implemented for 0.5.2: five dialect name pools, authored ruling
families and adult court members. Native cabinet recruitment draws on these names.

## A. Clipped and brutal

Mostly one or two syllables, heavy stops, readable spellings and few apostrophes.
Examples: Grakka, Drogg, Morga, Kragg, Brugha, Throkk.
Strongest fit for blunt, combative goblins; less distinction between cultures.

## B. Hissing and jagged

Mostly two syllables with sk, kr, vr, zz and sh. Examples: Skritcha, Krizzek,
Vrezka, Zrikka, Snikh, Vrakzha. Strongest fit for scheming, frantic goblins;
using this sound everywhere could make characters difficult to distinguish.

Use **personal name + house name**, with a separate optional earned epithet.
Personal names are normally one to three syllables. Avoid a mandatory ending
such as -kin, and avoid using punctuation as the main source of distinction.
Names should sound related across the islands without being interchangeable.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Emberblood / Cindermaw | g, k, gr, hard stops | Grakka, Kragga, Mograz | Drogg, Grask, Korgat | Ashjaw, Ironbite, Cindermaw |
| Brineward / Brackmaw | br, m, gh, longer low vowels | Brugha, Morga, Ghazra | Murgash, Ghorr, Brumak | Brackmaw, Miretooth, Reedclaw |
| Reefstrider / Reefhook | sk, z, ik, quick syllables | Zikka, Skrizza, Rizzka | Skrezz, Krizzek, Zikrat | Reefhook, Shoalcut, Shellsplit |
| Stormfang / Shatterfin | vr, sh, kr, sharp endings | Vrazka, Skritcha, Rikkra | Vrosh, Krashik, Sharg | Shatterfin, Knifeback, Stormscar |
| Ashveil / Sootwake | s, zh, kh, softer endings | Zheska, Khazra, Sishka | Snikh, Zhor, Khash | Sootwake, Cinderhush, Blackreed |

Gender examples are illustrative, not mandatory suffix rules. Some names may be
shared. Members of one culture can belong to different houses; country names are
not imposed as the surname of every inhabitant. Unrelated generated characters
should not all be assigned to the ruling dynasty.

## Houses, titles and maternal inheritance

- A **house name** is a stable dynasty name, not an earned nickname.
- Shatterfin transmits its ruling house through mothers under its special law.
  The other clans retain their existing dynasty rules and strongest-male succession.
- An **epithet** is optional: Saltfang, Chainbiter, Ash-Eater, Hookhand, Reef-Cutter.
- **Tidemother** is Shatterfin's ruler title, not every Stormfang woman's title.
- Full formal example: **Tidemother Jaima Shatterfin, the Mare-Mother**.
- Everyday example: **Jaima Shatterfin**. The nickname must not replace the
  dynasty field or create a new dynasty for her children.
