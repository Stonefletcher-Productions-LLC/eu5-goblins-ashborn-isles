# Goblins of the Ashborn Isles 0.6.3

## Construction economy and Jaimzha

- Jaima is now **Jaimzha, the Mare-Mother**. Her character identity, family and succession remain intact.
- Every goblin crown starts with masonry, glass, tools, cloth, leather, paper, pottery and naval-supply industries.
- Mason levels rise from 6 to 30: Cindermaw 8, Brackmaw 6, Shatterfin 5, Reefhook 3, Sootwake 3 and Giltfang 5. Glass guilds rise from 5 to 13.
- Added sand at Reedfish and Tidefang, expanded planned sand/stone capacity, and supplied Giltfang with fiber farms, charcoal and a weapon guild.
- New jobs retain the 125% native staffing target and a separate RGO reserve. All 97 district populations, minorities, slaves and roughly 25% tribal shares are preserved; total population remains 1,895,000.
- Native production-chain checks include Portugal as a scale reference. Actual hiring, market membership, food and profitability remain unverified in game.

**EU5 1.3.11. Start a new 1337 campaign.** Close EU5, extract the complete installer ZIP, run Install-Goblins.cmd, and enable only one Goblins copy.

The population, Wildfang Clans, Highfangs, portraits, diplomacy, voyages and other features from 0.6.2 remain included. This is a new release; the original v0.6.2 download is preserved.
